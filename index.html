Play Quiz Master Online

Quiz Master is a free bilingual quiz game for players who enjoy learning, testing general knowledge and improving their speed. Choose a category, answer 10 timed questions and build your score.

What can you play?

Try General Knowledge, Science, History, Geography, Sports, Entertainment, Technology, Bangladesh and Math. The game supports English and বাংলা, so you can switch language before or during a quiz.

Daily Quiz and achievements

Come back each day for a 10-question Daily Quiz and a bonus reward. Your best score, total score, games played and achievements are saved in your browser.

Built for mobile

The interface is designed for phones and works in modern web browsers. You can play directly online without installing a separate app.

Is Quiz Master free?

Yes. The core quiz game is free to play.

Can I play in Bangla?

Yes. Use the language button at the top to switch between English and বাংলা.

How many questions are available?

The current question bank contains 220+ bilingual questions across multiple categories.
